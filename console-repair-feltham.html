🔌 HDMI Port Repair - Most Common Console Problem

Is your console not displaying on TV? HDMI port broken? HDMI port damage is the #1 most common console repair issue. Whether it's physical damage, bent pins, or complete port failure, we can fix it!

⚠️ Signs of HDMI Port Damage:
  • No signal on TV
  • Intermittent picture (works sometimes)
  • HDMI cable won't stay in
  • Loose HDMI connection
  • Black screen but console powers on
  • Sparkly/snowy screen
  • Resolution problems
  • Bent or broken pins visible

❓ Console Repair FAQs

How long does a console repair take?
Most console repairs are completed the same day! Controller drift repairs typically take 1-2 hours, HDMI port repairs take 2-3 hours, disc drive repairs 2-4 hours, and overheating/cleaning services 1-2 hours. More complex repairs like APU reballing or YLOD/RROD fixes may take 1-2 days. We'll always give you an estimated timeframe upfront.
Can you fix PS5/PS4 HDMI port issues?
Yes, absolutely! HDMI port repair is one of our most common console repairs. Whether your PS5/PS4 has no signal to the TV, a loose HDMI connection, bent pins, or complete port failure, we can fix it. PS4 HDMI port repair costs £59.99 and PS5 costs £69.99. If the HDMI IC chip is also damaged, that's an additional repair. Most HDMI repairs are completed same-day with a 3-month warranty.
Can you fix Nintendo Switch Joy-Con drift?
Yes! Joy-Con drift is extremely common and we fix it regularly. The repair costs £39.99 per Joy-Con and takes 1-2 hours. We replace the faulty analog stick with a high-quality replacement and thoroughly test it. This is much cheaper than buying new Joy-Cons at £65+ for a pair! We also fix Switch Pro Controller drift, Switch charging ports, cracked screens, and all other Switch issues.
Is it worth repairing an old console like PS3 or Xbox 360?
It depends on the issue and your attachment to the console! For common problems like YLOD (Yellow Light of Death) on PS3 or RROD (Red Ring of Death) on Xbox 360, repairs start from £59.99. If you have games you still want to play or save data you want to preserve, it's definitely worth repairing. However, if the console has multiple major failures, upgrading to a newer generation might make more sense. We'll give you honest advice about whether repair or replacement is better for your situation.
Do you test consoles after repair?
Absolutely! We thoroughly test every console repair with actual games before returning it to you. For HDMI repairs, we test display output; for disc drives, we test disc reading and loading; for overheating issues, we run stress tests; for controllers, we test every button and stick in games. Your console is only returned when it's working perfectly and we're confident it'll perform well for you.
What warranty do you offer on console repairs?
We provide a 3-month warranty on all console repairs covering both parts and labor. If the same issue reoccurs within 3 months, we'll fix it again at no charge. This warranty demonstrates our confidence in the quality of our repairs and the parts we use. Note that the warranty doesn't cover new issues, accidental damage, or water damage that occurs after the repair.
Can you fix a console that won't turn on?
Yes, in most cases! A console that won't power on can have several causes: faulty power supply, damaged power button, motherboard issues, or short circuits. We offer a free diagnostic to identify the exact problem. Repairs start from £49.99 depending on the issue. Common causes like power supply failures are relatively easy to fix, while motherboard damage may be more complex. We'll diagnose it and give you an honest assessment of the repair cost and chances of success.
