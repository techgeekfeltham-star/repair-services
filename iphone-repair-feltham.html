❓ iPhone Repair FAQs

How long does iPhone repair take?
Most iPhone repairs (screen, battery, charging port) take 30-60 minutes. Water damage repairs may take 24-48 hours for proper drying and cleaning. We offer same-day service for most repairs.
Will my data be safe during repair?
Yes! We don't need your passwords or passcodes for hardware repairs. Your data remains on your iPhone and is never accessed, viewed, or copied. All your photos, messages, and apps stay completely private and safe.
Do you use genuine Apple parts?
We use high-quality parts that meet or exceed Apple's specifications. Our screens feature the same technology as original displays - proper touch sensitivity, accurate colors, and durability. All parts come with warranty.
What warranty do you offer?
We provide 3-month warranty on screen repairs, 6-month warranty on battery replacements, and standard warranty on all other hardware repairs. For water damage repairs: the 3-month warranty covers any replacement parts we install (such as charging port, battery, etc.), but not the water damage repair service itself, as water damage can have unpredictable long-term effects. All warranties cover parts and labor. If anything goes wrong with our repair, we fix it free of charge.
Can you repair older iPhone models?
Yes! We repair all iPhone models from iPhone 6 onwards. We keep parts in stock for most models. Even for discontinued iPhones, we can usually source parts within 24 hours.
How much cheaper are you than Apple Store?
Our repairs typically cost 40-60% less than Apple Store prices. For example, an iPhone screen repair at Apple might cost £200+, while we charge from £24.99-£160 depending on the model. Same-day service included!
Do I need to book an appointment?
No appointment necessary! Walk in Monday-Friday 9am-7pm, Saturday-Sunday 10am-6pm. However, calling ahead (020 8890 6161) ensures we have your specific parts ready and can give you an accurate time estimate.
