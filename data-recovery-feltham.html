❓ Data Recovery FAQs

What are my chances of data recovery?
It depends on the type of damage. For accidental deletion or formatting: 90-95% success rate. For logical failures: 80-90%. For physical damage: 60-80%. Water damage: 50-70%. We provide honest assessment after FREE evaluation.
How long does data recovery take?
Standard recovery: 3-7 days. Simple cases: 24-48 hours. Emergency service: 24-48 hours (express processing). Complex physical recoveries: 7-14 days. We provide estimated timeframe after assessment.
Is my data safe and private?
Absolutely! We treat all data with strict confidentiality. We never view, copy, or retain your personal files. Data is recovered directly to your new device or storage. All work is done securely at our Feltham location with full privacy protection.
What if you can't recover my data?
For most standard services, we operate "No Data, No Fee" - if we can't recover your data, you don't pay the recovery fee (assessment is always free). We're honest about recovery chances from the start.
Should I try recovery software myself first?
NO! Using recovery software improperly can overwrite data and make professional recovery impossible. If data is important, bring it to us first. We use professional tools that won't cause further damage. DIY attempts often reduce recovery chances significantly.
What should I do if I've lost data?
Stop using the device immediately! Don't save new files, don't install software, don't try DIY recovery. Turn it off if possible. Bring it to us as soon as possible. Quick action improves recovery chances significantly.
