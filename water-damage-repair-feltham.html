⚠️ What To Do If Your Phone Gets Wet:
1. Turn it off immediately - Don't try to turn it on
2. Remove the case - Take off any protective case
3. Remove SIM card - Take out SIM and SD cards if possible
4. Don't use rice! - This doesn't work and can cause more damage
5. Bring it to us ASAP - Time is critical for successful recovery
6. Don't charge it - Charging can cause short circuits

❓ Water Damage Repair FAQs

How much does water damage repair cost?
£45 for all phones, all models. This includes professional labour, ultrasonic deep cleaning, complete drying, corrosion removal, and full diagnostic testing. If your phone requires replacement parts (charging port, battery, screen, etc.), these are charged separately at our standard parts pricing. We provide a clear quote before proceeding with any parts replacement.
How quickly should I bring my water-damaged phone in?
Immediately! The sooner we can start the repair process, the better the chances of full recovery. Water damage worsens over time as corrosion spreads. Bring it in as soon as possible - even if it's outside business hours, call us for emergency guidance.
Will you be able to save my photos and data?
In most cases, yes! Even if the phone doesn't power on, we can often recover data from the storage chip. We don't need your password - we work on the hardware level. Your data security is maintained throughout the process.
What should I NOT do after water damage?
Don't: turn it on, charge it, use a hairdryer, put it in rice, press buttons repeatedly, or shake it. DO: turn it off, remove the case and SIM, pat it dry gently, and bring it to us immediately.
How long does water damage repair take?
Most repairs take 24-48 hours for proper drying and cleaning. However, we offer same-day service for emergency cases where possible. The diagnostic is done immediately while you wait.
What if my phone can't be repaired?
If full repair isn't possible, we focus on data recovery. We can often extract your photos, contacts, and important files to transfer to a new device. We'll always be honest about what's recoverable and give you all available options.
Do you offer any warranty?
Yes! We provide a 3-month warranty on any replacement parts we install during the water damage repair (such as charging port, battery, screen, etc.). Please note: The warranty covers the parts installed only, not the water damage repair service itself. Water damage can have unpredictable long-term effects, so we cannot warranty against future water damage issues. However, we stand behind the quality of any parts we replace.
How much does the diagnostic cost?
The diagnostic is completely FREE with no obligation. We'll assess the damage, tell you exactly what needs to be done, and provide a clear quote. You only pay if you proceed with the repair.
