❓ PC Repair FAQs

How much does it cost to fix a slow computer?
Our PC Speed Up service costs £34.99 and includes deep system cleanup, removal of junk files, startup optimization, registry cleaning, and driver updates. If your PC needs virus removal as well, we charge £34.99 for virus removal. The £45 diagnostic charge applies to all services and covers our professional assessment, labour, and any parts needed. Most slow PC issues are fixed the same day, and your computer will feel like new again!
Can you build me a custom gaming PC?
Absolutely! We specialize in custom gaming PC builds. We'll work with you to understand your gaming needs, budget, and favorite games, then recommend the perfect components. Prices start from £599 for budget gaming PCs up to £2,000+ for high-end enthusiast builds. All our gaming PCs include professional assembly, cable management, Windows 11, RGB lighting setup, and full testing. Build time is typically 3-5 days depending on parts availability.
How long does a PC repair take?
Most PC repairs are completed the same day! Virus removal typically takes 2-3 hours, PC speed up takes 2-4 hours, and component upgrades like RAM or SSD installation take 1-2 hours. More complex repairs like motherboard replacement or data recovery may take 1-3 days. We'll always give you an estimated timeframe upfront.
Is upgrading my PC cheaper than buying a new one?
Usually, yes! Upgrading key components like adding an SSD (£44.99 for 500GB) and more RAM (£79.99 for 16GB) can dramatically improve performance for under £170, compared to £500+ for a new PC. Plus our £45 diagnostic charge includes labour and parts if needed. However, if your PC is more than 7-8 years old, buying new might make more sense. We'll assess your current PC and give honest advice about whether upgrading or replacing is the better option.
Can you recover my deleted files or data from a crashed hard drive?
Yes! We offer data recovery services starting from £49.99. We can recover deleted files, data from formatted drives, corrupted files, and even data from failed hard drives. Our success rate is over 85%. We provide a free assessment first - if we can't recover your data, you don't pay anything. Common recoverable items include photos, videos, documents, emails, and more.
Do you repair Mac computers and MacBooks?
Yes! We repair all Mac computers including iMacs, MacBook Pro, MacBook Air, Mac Mini, and Mac Pro. Common Mac repairs include SSD upgrades, RAM upgrades, screen replacements, battery replacements, keyboard repairs, and software issues. Mac repairs may take slightly longer than PC repairs due to their more complex design, but we have the tools and expertise needed.
What's the best upgrade to make my PC faster?
The single best upgrade is an SSD (Solid State Drive). If your PC currently has a traditional hard drive, upgrading to an SSD will make it 10x faster! Boot times go from minutes to seconds, programs open instantly, and overall responsiveness improves dramatically. Our 500GB SSD upgrade costs £44.99 (plus £45 diagnostic charge which includes installation and Windows transfer). The second best upgrade is adding more RAM, especially if you have less than 16GB.
