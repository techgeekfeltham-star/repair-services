❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a screen repair take?
Most screen repairs are completed within 30 minutes. More complex repairs or older models may take up to 1 hour. We'll give you an accurate time estimate when you bring your phone in.
Will I lose my data during screen replacement?
No! Screen replacement doesn't affect your data at all. We don't need your passwords for screen replacement - your device remains locked throughout the repair. All your photos, apps, contacts, and settings remain completely safe. We never access or view your personal information.
What warranty do you offer on screen repairs?
We provide a comprehensive 3-month warranty on all screen repairs, covering both parts and labor. If you experience any issues with the replacement screen within 3 months, we'll fix it free of charge.
Do I need to book an appointment?
No appointment necessary! We operate on a walk-in basis Monday to Friday 9am-7pm, Saturday-Sunday 10am-6pm. However, if you'd like to reserve a time slot during busy periods, you can call us on 020 8890 6161.
What type of screens do you use?
We use premium quality replacement screens that meet or exceed original manufacturer specifications. All screens are tested for touch sensitivity, color accuracy, and durability before fitting.
Can you repair phones with water damage and cracked screens?
Yes! We offer comprehensive water damage repair alongside screen replacement. We'll diagnose the full extent of damage and provide a complete quote for all necessary repairs.
Do you match competitors' prices?
Absolutely! We offer a price match guarantee. If you find a lower price from a registered UK repair shop within 5 miles of Feltham, we'll match it. Just show us their written quote.
What if my phone has other damage besides the screen?
We'll conduct a free diagnostic and identify all issues. We can repair multiple problems at once, often at a discounted combined price. Common additional issues include battery problems, charging port damage, and button failures.
