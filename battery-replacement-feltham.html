⚡ Tips to Extend Your New Battery Life:

  • Avoid charging overnight - unplug when it reaches 100%
  • Keep your phone between 20-80% charge when possible
  • Avoid extreme temperatures (too hot or too cold)
  • Close apps running in the background
  • Lower screen brightness when not needed
  • Use original or certified chargers only
  • Enable battery optimization features in settings
