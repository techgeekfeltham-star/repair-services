💧 Samsung Water Damage? Act Fast!

Dropped your Samsung in water, toilet, or any liquid? Time is critical! The sooner we can start the repair process, the better the chances of saving your device and recovering your data.

🚨 What NOT to Do:
  • ❌ Don't try to turn it on or charge it
  • ❌ Don't put it in rice (this doesn't work!)
  • ❌ Don't use a hairdryer or heat source
  • ❌ Don't press buttons repeatedly
  • ❌ Don't wait - corrosion spreads quickly!
✅ What TO Do:
  • ✓ Power off immediately (if still on)
  • ✓ Remove SIM and SD cards if possible

❓ Samsung Repair FAQs

What warranty do you offer on Samsung repairs?
We provide a 3-month warranty on screen repairs and a 6-month warranty on battery replacements. All other hardware repairs come with our standard warranty. For water damage repairs: the 3-month warranty covers any replacement parts we install (such as charging port, camera, battery, etc.), but not the water damage repair service itself, as water damage can have unpredictable long-term effects. All warranties cover both parts and labor.
How long does a Samsung repair take?
Most Samsung repairs are completed the same day! Screen replacements typically take 30-45 minutes, battery changes 20-30 minutes, and charging port repairs 30-45 minutes. Water damage repairs may take 2-4 hours for proper cleaning and drying. Complex repairs like motherboard work or foldable screens may require 24-48 hours.
Do you repair Samsung foldable phones (Z Fold & Z Flip)?
Yes! We specialize in Samsung foldable phone repairs. We can fix both the inner flexible display and outer screens on Z Fold and Z Flip models. We also repair hinges, install screen protectors, and address crease issues. Foldable repairs require specialist knowledge and tools - which we have!
Will my Samsung data be safe during the repair?
100% yes! We don't need your passwords, PINs, or patterns for hardware repairs. Your photos, messages, apps, and personal data remain completely private and secure. We work only on the hardware level and never access your personal information. However, we always recommend backing up your data as a precaution before any repair.
Do you use original Samsung parts?
We use high-quality replacement parts specifically designed for Samsung devices. For screens, we offer both OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) AMOLED displays and premium aftermarket options, depending on your budget and preferences. All our parts are thoroughly tested and come with warranty coverage. We'll always discuss part options with you before starting the repair.
What's the difference between A Series and S Series charging port prices?
A Series phones (budget/mid-range like A15, A25, A35, A54) have simpler charging port assemblies and are easier to replace, so we charge £29.99. S Series phones (premium flagships like S21, S22, S23, S24) have more complex assemblies with additional features and require more intricate work, so the price is £39.99. Both include the same quality service and warranty.
My Samsung got wet - can you save it?
Bring it in immediately! The sooner we can start the water damage repair process, the better your chances. Our £45 water damage service includes ultrasonic cleaning, complete drying, corrosion removal, and diagnostic testing. We successfully save many water-damaged Samsung phones. Even if your device won't turn on, we can often recover your photos, contacts, and data. Don't wait - corrosion spreads quickly!
